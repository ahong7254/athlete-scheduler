Study blocks and breaks

The question: today the scheduler can give one subject up to 8h in a day and lays each subject out as a single unbroken block (the stress test found 400+ blocks longer than 4h). How big should a study block be, how often should breaks come, and what should happen to hours that no longer fit? Below is one evening (after dinner until wind-down) with the hours the optimizer asked for. Four candidate rules lay it out side by side. Breaks are counted as real time: either the plan budgets for them, or they push bedtime later.
