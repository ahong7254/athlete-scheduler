The evening after a late practice

The question: the optimizer budgets hours per day but not clock order. On a practice day, whatever didn't fit before practice (homework, study, dinner) has to happen after it, and wind-down comes last. When practice is late or long, the evening runs past lights-out and the night gets shorter than planned (2% of random days, and the real Oct 12 Thursday). When the evening doesn't fit, what should give way? This replays one school-day afternoon and evening the way the scheduler lays it out, under four candidate rules. Sleep is counted from lights-out + 20 min to fall asleep until the next wake-up.

The day

Practice length, homework and study are what the optimizer chose for the day. The "practice-day floor" (1h) and the school-day homework minimum (1.5h) still apply when a rule trims.
