Training minimum vs. protected days

The question: the scheduler has a weekly training minimum (swim + weights ≥ 40% of 14h = 5.6h). It also has protected days: the recovery check (back off / take it easier) and events (travel, big test, rest day) put a soft cap on practice, and "sick" is a hard zero. Today the minimum wins: when protected days leave too few hours, the plan pushes practice back up — sometimes on the very days it was told to protect.
